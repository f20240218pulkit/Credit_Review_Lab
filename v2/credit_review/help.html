Workspace help

Prepare a statement pack

Use a text-based PDF with two reporting years, a supported fiscal-year heading, and amounts in INR. A pack can be up to 2 MiB and 10 pages. Scans and password-protected PDFs are unsupported. For manually transcribed values, upload a UTF-8 CSV with the header year,label,value,unit,currency. Use two years, INR, and units or thousands. The workspace keeps the uploaded CSV and generates a citeable evidence view.

Download a sample statement pack

Review the evidence

Open a case and select a source reference to see the statement line. Confirm the account mapping or exclude a competing line with a reason. Review outstanding checks before preparing a memo.

Fix a failed upload

Check the file format and the limits above. If extraction still fails, share the error text with your workspace administrator. Keep borrower documents out of support messages.
